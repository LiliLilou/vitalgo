Your Questions About Energy After 50

What vitamins should a woman over 50 take daily?

Many women over 50 look at vitamin B12, vitamin D3, magnesium and iron, because needs and absorption change with age. The right mix depends on your diet and health, so our 2-minute quiz and your doctor are the best place to start.

What is the best energy supplement for women over 50?

Look for a formula made for your life stage, with clear doses of key nutrients like B12 and iron, third-party testing and no artificial fillers. Our Daily Energy Multi 50+ was built around exactly those criteria.

Why am I always tired after 50?

Shifting hormones, lower B12 absorption and restless nights are common reasons women feel more tired after 50. Lifestyle, stress and some health conditions also play a role, so persistent fatigue is worth discussing with your doctor.

Can vitamins help with menopause fatigue?

Vitamins are not a cure, but nutrients like B12, iron and magnesium support normal energy metabolism and can be part of a routine that also includes sleep, movement and good food.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Every body is different. Many customers tell us they notice a change within 2 to 4 weeks of daily use, which is why we offer a 60-day money-back guarantee.

Is VitalGo safe to take with my medication?

Our formulas use well-researched ingredients in clear doses, but some nutrients can interact with medication. Please check with your doctor or pharmacist before starting any new supplement.
